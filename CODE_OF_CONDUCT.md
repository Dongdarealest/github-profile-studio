# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a welcoming experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background or identity. This is a
small, friendly project about making profiles beautiful — keep it kind.

## Our standards

Welcome beginners. Answer the question that was asked. Critique designs and
code, never people. Assume good faith, and leave every thread a little more
helpful than you found it.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
anyone's private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly,
and maintainers may remove content or contributors who break these standards.
